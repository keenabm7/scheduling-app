# Ulluriaq Scheduling App — Feature Specification

**Organization:** Ulluriaq Adolescent Center (UTHC) — Ungava Tulattavik Health Center
**Purpose:** Complete developer handoff specification for building the UTHC workforce scheduling application.
**Live prototype:** https://claude.ai/artifact/NaCK1Mj3exjsN87CGXeDd8

---

## 1. Overview

A web-based Progressive Web App (PWA) that replaces manual/paper scheduling for Ulluriaq's two units — **Boys** and **Girls** — each staffed by **Educators** and **Intervention Agents (IAs)** across day, evening, and overnight shifts, tracked in two-week **Pay Periods**.

Two user roles:
- **Management & Scheduler** — full edit access to all schedules, staff records, approvals, and restricted information.
- **Employee** — read-only access to schedules, personal requests, and messaging.

There is no "Admin" label anywhere in the product. Use "Scheduling Admin" or "Unit Manager" instead.

| Session persistence | Sessions persist between the 12-month OTP checkpoints. |
| Management landing page | Management Dashboard (Overview tab). |
| Employee landing page | Their own pinned schedule. |
| Suspend / Terminate / Reinstate | Management can set any employee to Active, Suspended, or Terminated. Suspended/Terminated = immediate login block. Reinstate = immediate access restoration. Historical data retained regardless of status. Each change supports an optional note and effective date. |

## 3. Schedules (Core Feature)

### 3.1 The Six Schedules
1. Boys — Educators
2. Boys — Intervention Agents (Day/Evening)
3. Boys — Intervention Agents (Overnight)
4. Girls — Educators
5. Girls — Intervention Agents (Day/Evening)
6. Girls — Intervention Agents (Overnight)

### 3.2 Layout & Time Range
- Full **4-week view**: current Pay Period (Weeks 1–2) + next Pay Period (Weeks 3–4), stacked vertically on one scrollable screen.
- All employees on the relevant roster appear as rows; dates as columns.
- Staff name column: compact (~96–110px) to maximize horizontal grid space.
- Sticky headers: date row and name column remain visible while scrolling.
- Schedules are only visible to employees once officially published by management (confidentiality rule — staff cannot see templates before they are finalized).

### 3.3 Shift Codes
J1, J2, J/T, S1, S2, S/T, N, N/T, N/E, T, R, V, DNR, SW, OT, CNR, TRAN, ESC — each color-coded per Section 18.

### 3.4 Cell Editing (Management only)
Clicking any shift cell opens an edit panel with:
- Set/change shift code
- Add sw (switch) or v (vacation) tag
- Mark as overtime (highlights staff name in baby blue)
- Assign a cover/fill-in name — appears below the shift code on the grid for all viewers. Options: all UTHC staff + agency staff (agency names marked with *)
- Clear the cell

### 3.5 Transportation & Escort (TRAN / ESC)
- Shown on the shared schedule grid.
- Clicking opens a linked memo with: number of youth, destination/community, start time, end time, notes.
- **Visibility:** assigned employee + management only. All other employees see the code but get an "access restricted" message on click.
- A summary section at the bottom of each schedule (or as a linked section) lists all TRAN/ESC entries: staff name, date, times, community.

### 3.6 Loan of Service (LOS)
- **Not shown on the shared schedule grid.**
- Tracked as duration of stay: originating unit, receiving unit, departure date, return date, auto-calculated duration.
- Visible only to: (a) the employee on loan (personal view) and (b) Management (dedicated tab).

### 3.7 Last-Minute Cover (under 2 hours)
When a book-off is submitted with under 2 hours to shift start:
- The system first shows employees **currently scheduled and working** as the first offer option (labeled "Currently scheduled — working").
- Then lists staff available as per their availability sheet and rotation order.
- Management is notified immediately.

## 5. Employee Experience

- Lands on their pinned home schedule after login.
- Can switch between all 4 schedule tabs; one is set as the default home via a pin control.
- Schedules are **read-only**. Clicking a cell shows shift info and prompts toward a request flow.
- Employees can view: their own schedule, their own book-offs, total regular shifts, total overtime shifts, their own availability submission time, the on-call manager for the current week.
- Employees can **never** see: Management Dashboard, other staff's leave details, LOS entries for others, TRAN/ESC details for shifts they are not assigned to, or schedule templates before official publication.

## 6. Overtime (OT) Distribution

Priority order, strictly enforced:
1. Full-time employees — by seniority (most senior offered first). No junior FT staff may accumulate more OT than a senior FT staff member.
2. Part-time employees — by rotation/seniority.
3. Recall workers.
4. Agency (if all above decline or are unavailable).

Each offer:
- **T (Taken):** Email confirmation to employee + management. Cover name appears on the schedule. Cell marked T.
- **R (Refused):** Email with date/time to employee + management. Logged. System auto-advances to next person in rotation. Cell marked R.

Management must always see who was last offered/given a shift to verify fair rotation. The app must indicate on the schedule view who is available for a given day as per their availability sheet (management view only), in rotation order, so management can verify before offering.

All regular time and overtime shift totals must be visible to both employee and management.

## 7. Requests & Approvals

### 7.1 Book-off / Vacation Requests (Employee-submitted)
- Employee selects date(s) and a **mandatory** leave-type tag before they can submit: Sick, Vacation, Stat, Psychosocial, LIB, Maternity leave. Submission is blocked until one is selected.
- **Under 24 hours:** auto-denied with in-app message: "This request is less than 24 hours — please contact management directly." Management notified of the attempt.
- **Under 2 hours:** see §3.7 (last-minute cover logic).
- **3+ consecutive days:** auto-flagged as vacation, requires management approval.
- **3 consecutive weekend (Fri/Sat/Sun) book-offs:** automatic management alert.
- Management approves/denies from the Book-offs tab. Both outcomes notify the employee via app + email.
- Employee view includes a history of all their own book-offs and statuses.

### 7.2 Shift Switches — 3-Step Chain
1. **Proposal:** Employee A messages Employee B and/or sends a formal switch proposal specifying: shift they give up, shift they would receive, date, unit.
2. **Counterparty agreement:** Employee B agrees or declines in the app. If declined, process ends. Never reaches management.
3. **Management approval:** Once both agree, the request appears in management's approval queue for final approval or denial.

Rules:
- Three-way switches are **never** permitted.
- Educator ↔ Educator only. IA ↔ IA only.
- Cross-unit (SWB/SWG) is permitted with unit indicated.
- Every stage sends in-app notification AND email to all relevant parties.
- Approved switches update the schedule automatically.

## 10. Messaging

- Direct messaging between any two employees.
- Primary use: coordinating shift switches before formalizing.
- "Propose switch" action inside a conversation launches the structured switch-proposal form (§7.2) and posts a live status card in the thread showing: Employee A agreed → Employee B agreed/pending → Management approved/pending.
- Unread message indicators on the conversation list.

---

## 11. Mass Communications

- Management/Scheduler can broadcast to: All staff, a specific unit, a specific role, or Management only.
- All sent broadcasts are logged and accessible to management for reference.
- Management can edit the wording of any automatic system message templates (e.g., the late availability message, the <24hr book-off denial message).

---

## 12. On-Call Manager

- All employees can see the on-call manager for the current week/weekend, with contact info.
- Automatic notification to all staff if the on-call assignment changes.
- Management can edit the assignment.
- **On-call manager's view includes:** all schedules, full seniority list, staff contact directory. Management can edit this area.
- Routing: Mon–Fri 9:00–17:00 → Scheduling Admin. After 17:00 + weekends → On-call manager.

---

## 13. Seniority Lists

- Separate lists for Educators and Intervention Agents.
- Each row: rank number, employee name, employment status (FT/PT/TFT/Recall), seniority in years and days.
- **Reorderable by management:** drag-and-drop or up/down arrow controls.
- Saved order directly drives OT distribution and rotation.

---

## 14. Staff Directory / Contact List

- Full staff list: name, role, employment status, unit, phone, email.
- Searchable and filterable by unit, role, employment status.
- Editable by management.
- Available to the on-call manager within the on-call package view.

## 15. Availability Sheets

- **Recall workers:** must submit every pay period to receive shifts.
- **Part-time block workers:** only required to submit if requesting shifts beyond their fixed block.
- **Submission window:** up to 10 days before the new schedule period begins (the Monday start date).
- **Deadline:** every second-to-last Wednesday of the month at 9:00 AM.
- **Late submissions:** placed at the bottom of the rotation. System sends automatic notification: *"Due to your availability being late, you have been placed at the bottom of the rotation."* Email also sent to management and scheduler.
- **Availability changes:** employees may request to add or remove a shift from their availability at any time, but changes only take effect 10 days after the request. Immediate notifications sent to the employee, management, and scheduler.
- **Non-compliant sheets** (below minimum requirements): system flags and notifies management; management decides whether to deny.

**Minimum requirements:**
- Recall: at least 4 shifts including 2 weekend shifts (Sat + Sun) and 1 evening weekend shift.
- PT block: already required to work 4 shifts per period with 2 weekend shifts (Sat + Sun).

**Sheet codes:**
- X = desired shift
- W = scheduled block shift (PT workers only)

**Viewing:**
- Employees, schedulers, and management can all view each employee's availability sheet.
- The app shows management which employees are available for a given open shift, in rotation order, based on their availability sheet — management view only.

## 16. Notifications

Every action below triggers both **in-app notification AND email** to all relevant parties:

- Book-off submitted / approved / denied / attempted under 24hr
- Switch proposed / agreed / declined / approved / denied by management
- OT offered / taken / refused
- Schedule updated (live on the days indicated per §17)
- Account status changed (suspended / terminated / reinstated)
- Mass communication sent
- On-call assignment changed
- Availability sheet submitted / late / changed
- 3 consecutive weekend book-offs alert to management
- LOS assignment created or changed (management + affected employee only)

All automatic notification messages should be editable by management.

---

## 17. Schedule Updates — Timing & Routing

- Live schedule updates published on **Monday, Wednesday, and Friday**; update dates clearly indicated at the top of the schedule.
- Requests and updates received:
  - Mon–Fri 9:00–17:00 → Scheduling Admin
  - After 17:00 and weekends → On-call manager
- Employees see live schedule updates on those days in their app.

## 19. Non-Functional Requirements

- **Role-based visibility** is a first-class requirement — not an afterthought. Multiple features depend on "visible to: this specific employee + management" as a data model pattern.
- **Mobile-first responsive design** — staff are shift workers checking schedules from their phone.
- **Progressive Web App (PWA)** — installable on phone home screens, works offline to a degree, capable of push notifications without an App Store release.
- **Quebec Law 25 (Loi 25)** — confirm with CIUSSS IT/privacy contact whether provincial health-data privacy requirements apply to staff personal and leave data, and build accordingly.
- **Data retention** — all historical schedule and staff data is retained even after an employee is suspended or terminated.
- **Editable auto-messages** — management can edit the wording of all automatic system-generated messages.
- **On-call package** — the system must be able to send or display an on-call package to managers containing: seniority list, availability sheets, most updated schedules, and employee contact numbers.
- **Time adjustments** — management can make time adjustments (arrival/departure) and add internal notes on employees, visible to management only.

## 20. Suggested Phasing (for quoting purposes)

**Phase 1 — Core (minimum viable launch)**
- Authentication (incl. 12-month OTP, suspend/terminate)
- All 6 schedule grids (4-week view, full roster, editable by management, read-only for employees)
- Book-off requests + approval (incl. 24hr auto-deny)
- Staff directory + seniority lists (reorderable)
- Notifications (app + email) for the above

**Phase 2 — Operational depth**
- OT distribution queue and rotation logic
- Shift switch request chain (employee↔employee↔management)
- Schedule update timing and routing
- Staff on Leave tracker (incl. progressive return, restricted visibility)
- Transport & Escort memos (linked to TRAN/ESC cells)
- Last-minute cover logic (under 2 hours)
- 3 consecutive weekend book-off alert

**Phase 3 — Extended features**
- Coworker direct messaging
- Availability sheet digital submission, late detection, and rotation placement
- Loan of Service tracking
- Mass communications log with editable auto-message templates
- On-call manager assignment, on-call package view
- Staff directory contact screen (browsable by employees)
